Questions

FAQ.

  • How is Drops different from Foreplay or the Meta Ad Library?

    Foreplay and the Ad Library are dashboards you go look at. Drops is a short Slack thread each morning: yesterday’s winning vertical ads, already picked, with the landing-page and ad links.

  • What arrives in Slack?

    Search vertical, date, then three or four winning ads as video files plus LP link and ad link. Daily, not a weekly recap.

  • Is Drops the main product?

    No. ROI is the product: call payout from Ringba, CallGrid, TrackDrive, Retreaver, or Phonexa next to Meta, Google, and TikTok spend in Slack. Drops is the morning creative signal.
