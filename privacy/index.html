Legal Privacy

Privacy policy.

What we collect, why, and how to switch it off. Short version: read-only access, reports in your Slack, nothing sold.

Last updated __UPDATED_HUMAN__

The short version

  • ROI and Drops are read-only. We read ad spend and call payout. We never bid, pause, scale, or edit anything in your ad accounts or call platform.
  • We use your data to post your reports in your Slack. We do not sell it and we do not use it for advertising.
  • You can revoke our access at any time, from each platform or by emailing us.

Who we are

Easy Growth Ops is a project by Jamil Global (“we”, “us”). This policy covers this website and the two services we sell: ROI and Drops. Questions go to __CONTACT_EMAIL__.

What we collect

When you visit this website

  • Vercel Web Analytics. Page views and button clicks (for example, which “Book” or “Trial” button you pressed), with referrer, page, country, browser, and device type. Vercel Web Analytics does not use cookies.
  • Google Analytics 4, if we switch it on. Google receives the same page views and button clicks plus device and approximate location data, and sets first-party cookies to recognise returning browsers.
  • Calendly. Every booking and trial button opens Calendly. When you book, Calendly collects your name, email, and anything you type in the form, under Calendly’s own privacy policy, and shares it with us so we can run the call.

When you use ROI

  • Ad platforms (Meta Ads, Google Ads, TikTok Ads). Through read-only OAuth or API access you grant: ad account IDs and names, campaign names, and performance figures such as spend, clicks, and conversions.
  • Call platform (Ringba, CallGrid, TrackDrive, Retreaver, or Phonexa). Through a read-only API key or login you share: call counts, payout, revenue, fees, and the campaign names needed to match calls to spend. We use only the fields the report needs.
  • Slack. The workspace and channel you choose for delivery, and the messages our app posts there.

When you use Drops

  • The verticals you pick and the Slack workspace and channel for delivery.
  • The ads in each drop come from public sources such as ad libraries. They are not your data.

When you talk to us or pay us

  • Your name, company, email, and what you send us.
  • Billing details. Card payments are handled by our payment provider. We do not store full card numbers.

How we use it

  • To build and post your ROI and Drops updates in Slack.
  • To set up, support, and bill your account.
  • To see which pages and buttons work on this site, in aggregate.
  • To keep the services secure and fix problems.

We do not sell personal data. We do not use your ad or call data to advertise to you or to anyone else.

Data from Google, Meta, and TikTok

Access to ad platforms is read-only and used only to produce your reports. Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We follow the platform terms of Meta and TikTok for data received through their APIs. We do not sell this data, use it for advertising, or use it to train general-purpose AI models.

Who we share it with

Only with service providers that help us run Easy Growth Ops, and only for that purpose:

  • Slack, to deliver your updates.
  • Vercel, which hosts this website and runs Vercel Web Analytics.
  • Google, for Google Analytics 4 if it is switched on.
  • Calendly, for booking calls.
  • The infrastructure and automation providers that run the reporting jobs.
  • Our payment provider, for billing.

We may also disclose data if the law requires it, or to a buyer if Easy Growth Ops is sold, under the same protections as this policy.

Cookies

This site sets no cookies of its own. Vercel Web Analytics is cookieless. Google Analytics 4, when switched on, sets cookies. Calendly sets its own cookies on calendly.com.

How long we keep it

We keep account and report data while your subscription is active. When you cancel, we revoke or discard our access tokens and API keys and delete your stored report data within 30 days, unless the law requires us to keep something longer, such as invoices.

Security

Access tokens and API keys are limited to the people and systems that run your reports. No method of storage or transfer is perfectly secure, so we ask for read-only access only and you can revoke it at any time.

Revoking access and deleting data

  • Meta: Business Settings → Integrations / Apps, or remove our partner access.
  • Google Ads: remove our user or manager link in Google Ads, or remove app access in your Google Account’s third-party connections.
  • TikTok: remove our access in TikTok Business Center.
  • Call platform: delete or rotate the API key or user you shared with us.
  • Slack: remove the app from your workspace.

To get a copy of your data, correct it, or have it deleted, email __CONTACT_EMAIL__. Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK, or California), you may have further rights, including to object or to complain to a data protection authority. We will not treat you differently for using them.

International transfers

Our providers run in the United States and other countries. Where data moves across borders, we rely on the safeguards those providers offer, such as standard contractual clauses.

Children

Easy Growth Ops is for businesses. It is not meant for anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and, for material changes, tell customers in Slack or by email first.
