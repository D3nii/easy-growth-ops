RedTrack Pricing for Pay-Per-Call Buyers: What Each Plan Costs and What It Doesn’t Show

RedTrack’s affiliate and lead-gen plans run from $169 a month (Solo, monthly billing) to $999 (Enterprise). Annual billing is $118 to $699 a month. Prices are per RedTrack’s pricing page, 9 Nov 2026. The price covers tracking, not your call economics: call-platform fees and payout reconciliation sit outside it, so profit per call still needs your call platform’s data.

Prices checked Nov 9, 2026. Source: RedTrack pricing page, https://www.redtrack.io/pricing/.

Key facts

  • Monthly list prices as of 9 Nov 2026: Solo $169, Team $399, Enterprise $999. Custom is quoted on contact.
  • Annual prices on the page are Black Friday promo prices, about 30% below monthly. No end date is shown.
  • Faster ad-spend sync is a paid add-on priced by monthly ad spend. The page gives two different sets of add-on figures, and this page states both.
  • Events are RedTrack’s usage unit. The price doesn’t show call payout, call-platform fees, or profit per ad account.

The short answer

RedTrack’s price is a tracker price: event volume, seats, and how often ad spend syncs. Every paid plan lists Stop Rules, CAPI, MCP and API access, and day parting, plus a 14-day free trial with all add-ons unlocked (RedTrack’s listings, 9 Nov 2026). Annual prices on the page are Black Friday promo prices (about 30% below monthly), with no end date shown.

What the price doesn’t show is what a call paid, what the call platform kept, or what an ad account made after spend and fees. Match your event volume, team size, and sync speed to a plan, then compare that total against what your calls actually pay.

Monthly vs annual billing

As of 9 Nov 2026, RedTrack’s annual prices are Black Friday promo prices, about 30% below monthly (Solo $118 vs $169, Team $279 vs $399, Enterprise $699 vs $999). No promo end date is shown. The page also claims holiday pricing “up to 63% off” with no end date; treat that as a vendor claim. Terms listed either way: 14-day free trial with all add-ons unlocked, monthly or annual billing, no 12-month commitment, and a migration credit of up to 4 months off an annual plan.

Which plan fits your volume

Events and users

Events are RedTrack’s usage unit. Its compare table counts clicks, conversions, and postbacks. Whether a phone call counts as an event is not confirmed, so this page doesn’t map call volume to a plan. If you buy on calls, check how your call volume lands in RedTrack’s event count before you choose a plan from the table above.

Going over your event limit

Per RedTrack’s pricing FAQ (9 Nov 2026): nothing switches off. Overage is charged per 1,000 events (Solo $0.04, Team $0.035, Enterprise $0.025). If you stay above your allowance, RedTrack moves you to a matching plan next billing period. Confirm overage billing and which plan you land on after a sustained spike before you budget for one.

Ad accounts and data retention

Solo keeps 12 months of data and caps ad accounts at 5 per ad platform. Team keeps 24 months with unlimited ad accounts per platform. Enterprise keeps 36 months with unlimited ad accounts per platform. Extra Team and Enterprise seats are listed at $29 a month each.

Worked example: hypothetical buyer, not RedTrack or EGO data

Take a hypothetical buyer on Solo, billed annually, who records 6 million events in one month. Solo includes 5 million. The extra 1 million events at $0.04 per 1,000 come to $40. Added to the $118 a month annual price, the total is $158 a month. This is one way to add overage by hand from the published rates. It is not presented as RedTrack’s automatic billing, and we haven’t confirmed how RedTrack bills overage.

What the price leaves out for call buyers

RedTrack’s price covers the tracker: events, users, sync, and features. It doesn’t cover the call side of pay-per-call. Three things sit outside a plan price:

  • Call payout. What the call platform pays for each billable call.
  • Call-platform fees. The call platform’s own fees, such as Ringba’s or CallGrid’s.
  • Profit per ad account. Payout minus ad spend minus fees, for the same time window.

A tracker’s price and an account’s profit answer different questions. Some buyers need one, some need both. The claims that RedTrack ties Ringba and Retreaver calls to the click and sends call conversions back through CAPI come from our tracker comparison for pay-per-call (as of Oct 2026). They are not from RedTrack’s pricing page.

FAQ

  • How much does RedTrack cost per month?

    Solo is $169 monthly or $118 annual; Team is $399 or $279; Enterprise is $999 or $699. Annual prices are shown per month. As of 9 Nov 2026, the Black Friday 30% off applies to annual plans and add-ons during the promo. The page shows no end date. Ecommerce plans are $0 (Relay), $99 (Brand), and $599 (Agency) on monthly billing.

  • Does RedTrack’s price include call payout?

    No. The pricing page doesn’t mention calls, Ringba, call payout, or call-platform fees. Those sit outside the plan price.

  • Can RedTrack track pay-per-call calls?

    Yes, with a caveat. Per Easy Growth Ops’ tracker comparison for pay-per-call (as of Oct 2026), RedTrack ties Ringba and Retreaver calls to the click and sends call conversions back through CAPI. That claim isn’t on RedTrack’s pricing page; re-check it on RedTrack’s docs before you rely on it.

  • What happens if I go over my event limit?

    Nothing switches off. Overage is charged per 1,000 events (Solo $0.04, Team $0.035, Enterprise $0.025). If you stay above your limit, you move to a matching plan the next billing period, per RedTrack’s pricing FAQ checked 9 Nov 2026.

  • Is there a free trial, and does the Black Friday price hold on renewal?

    Yes on both, as RedTrack lists them on 9 Nov 2026. There’s a 14-day free trial with all add-ons unlocked, and no 12-month commitment. The banner says “You keep the Black Friday price when you renew.” Confirm that wording on the live page before you buy.
