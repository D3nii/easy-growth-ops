The formulas

These are the same definitions as the pay-per-call profit formula guide. Every row in the calculator uses them.

Calls
The calls you enter. In clicks mode: clicks × click-to-call rate ÷ 100, with no rounding.
Billable calls
Calls × billable rate ÷ 100.
Revenue (call payout)
Billable calls × payout per billable call.
Total cost
Ad spend + tool costs.
Net profit
Revenue − ad spend − tool costs.
ROI
Net profit ÷ total cost × 100. Can't be calculated when total cost is $0.
ROAS
(Revenue − ad spend) ÷ ad spend × 100. Ignores tool costs. 0% is break-even on ad spend. Needs ad spend above $0.
Revenue / ad spend
Revenue ÷ ad spend. This is how Google Ads shows ROAS, so 1.25x here is 125% in Google Ads.
Cost per call (CPC)
Ad spend ÷ calls. In ROI, CPC means cost per call.
Cost per billable call
Ad spend ÷ billable calls.
Break-even cost per call
(Revenue − tool costs) ÷ calls. The most you can pay per call, on ad spend alone, and still break even.
Break-even payout per billable call
Total cost ÷ billable calls. The payout per billable call you need to break even.

How to link or embed this calculator

Link to the calculator with a plain link. It is a normal page, so no iframe is needed:

__SITE_URL__/tools/pay-per-call-roi-calculator/

To link a scenario, put the inputs in the query string. This link opens the clicks version of the worked example above, with the same results:

__SITE_URL__/tools/pay-per-call-roi-calculator/?mode=clicks&spend=4000&clicks=2500&ctr=8&bill=62.5&payout=40&tools=300

The parameters are mode (calls or clicks), spend, calls, clicks, ctr (click-to-call rate), bill (billable rate), payout, and tools. A parameter you leave out uses the starting number. Anyone with the link can read the numbers in it.

FAQ

  • What is a good ROI for pay per call?

    There is no universal number. Start with break-even: the payout you need per billable call to cover ad spend and tool costs. Any ROI above 0% means the account made money after ad spend and tool costs. Whether that is enough depends on costs this calculator does not include, such as salaries and your time.

  • What is the difference between ROAS and ROI?

    ROAS compares revenue with ad spend only: (revenue − ad spend) ÷ ad spend. ROI compares net profit with total cost, which includes tool costs: net profit ÷ (ad spend + tool costs). Google Ads shows ROAS as revenue ÷ ad spend, so 125% there is 25% here. This calculator shows both.

  • What counts as a billable call?

    A billable call is one that meets the payout rules you are paid under, such as a minimum call length or a buffer the caller has to pass. Ask your buyer or check your call platform's report for the count. The billable rate is billable calls ÷ calls × 100.

  • How do I find break-even cost per call?

    Break-even cost per call = (revenue − tool costs) ÷ calls. It is the most you can pay per call, on ad spend alone, and still break even. Compare it with your actual cost per call, which is ad spend ÷ calls. If the actual figure is lower, the account is profitable.

  • Why enter payout per billable call instead of payout per call?

    Payout rules usually apply to billable calls, so billable calls × payout per billable call matches how revenue is counted. If you only know an average payout per call, enter 100% as the billable rate and that average as the payout. The math still works.

  • Can I share a scenario?

    Yes. The inputs go into the page address as you type. Use "Copy link to this scenario", and anyone who opens the link sees the same numbers. The math runs in your browser, there is no sign-up, and the link holds your numbers, so share it only with people who should see them.
