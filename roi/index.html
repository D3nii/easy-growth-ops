How I use it

I don’t keep Ads Manager open.

I’ve run almost $10 million in ad spend over the past three years. I can’t keep accounts open all day and sit there refreshing.

Google’s UI is late. The API is not. When I’m driving, at dinner, or away from the desk, Slack tells me if an account is profitable. I can pause a burst or scale a winner without opening a laptop for five minutes.

CPC and CPA sit in the same readout. I don’t switch tabs to check revenue or profit, or do the math every few minutes. That time goes back to the work.
